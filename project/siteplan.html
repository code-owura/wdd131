KickOff Central

A website about football (soccer) for new fans.

Site Name

KickOff Central

This name was chosen because "KickOff" represents the start of a football match — perfect for new fans beginning their journey into the sport. "Central" emphasizes that this site is a one-stop hub for football basics.

Site Purpose

KickOff Central is a beginner-friendly website that helps new football fans learn about the sport, discover famous clubs, and personalize their experience. The site provides:

  • A simple introduction to the rules and basics of football.
  • A gallery of famous football clubs from major leagues around the world.
  • A "My Favorites" feature that saves the user's preferred teams.

Scenarios

The following are questions that the target audience (new football fans) may ask:

  • What are the basic rules of football, and how is the game played?
  • Which are the most famous football clubs, and what leagues do they play in?
  • How can I save my favorite teams so I can revisit them later?
