What is Football?

Football, known as soccer in some parts of the world, is the most popular sport on the planet. Played by over 250 million players in more than 200 countries, it brings people together like no other sport. If you're new to football, you've come to the right place!

Football Information

⚽ Basic Rules

A football match is played between two teams of 11 players each. The goal is to score by getting the ball into the opposing team's net. Matches last 90 minutes, split into two halves.

🏆 Major Leagues

The biggest leagues include the English Premier League, Spanish La Liga, Italian Serie A, German Bundesliga, and French Ligue 1. Each features world-class teams and players.

🌍 World Cup

The FIFA World Cup is held every 4 years and is the most-watched sporting event in the world. It crowns the best national team on Earth.
